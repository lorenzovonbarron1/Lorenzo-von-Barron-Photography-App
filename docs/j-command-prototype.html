The new thing

The Rail — order by the song, not the seat

Every POS on earth models a venue as tables on a clock. Handlebar J is a room on a setlist. The Rail lets a guest choose where in the show their food lands — which hands the kitchen a known demand curve hours in advance instead of a wall of tickets at the break.

RED BOOT 4 Dance floor edge

When should it land?

92 dB and 8 lux. No reading, one thumb, haptic confirm. Production tiles carry photography, not type.

Pick an item and a landing window. Nothing fires until you do — and the runner finds you by zone and boot tag, not table number, because you're going to be dancing.
